Who we are


Grein-Eco Envirosafe Limited is a customer-focused alternative energy
company specializing in the marketing, trading, transportation, and
distribution of batteries, grid systems, wind turbines, and solar panels across
Nigeria and globally. Our vision is to lead in innovative environmental solutions,
promoting sustainability, delivering exceptional services, and fostering a
culture of environmental stewardship for a greener future.


Our Vision

Our Vision is to become the leading provider of innovative environmental solutions, fostering a sustainable future for our
